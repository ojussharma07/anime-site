Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Information We Collect

We believe in keeping your browsing experience as private as possible. Anime.TV does not require you to create an account, and we do not store your personal information on our servers.

2. Local Storage (Your History)

To provide features like "Continue Watching" and your "Watch History", we utilize your browser's Local Storage. This means your viewing history is saved directly on your own device, not on our servers. Clearing your browser data will erase this history.

3. Third-Party Services

We use third-party APIs (such as Jikan/MyAnimeList and TMDB) to fetch anime metadata, descriptions, and cover art. We also use third-party form providers (like Formspree) to handle our "Report a Bug" feature. When interacting with these features, basic network data (like IP addresses) may be visible to these third parties in accordance with their own privacy policies.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We advise you to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.
